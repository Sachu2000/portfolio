About Me

Technology has always been my biggest passion. I love building and assembling custom PCs, carefully researching every component, comparing hardware, and finding the right parts to create a system that's both powerful and well-balanced. I'm fascinated by the technology behind each component—from CPUs and GPUs to cooling solutions and storage.

I'm also an avid gamer, always ready to explore immersive worlds and experience great gameplay with friends. Beyond tech, motorcycles are a huge part of who I am. I love everything about them—from the engineering behind internal combustion engines and the precision of their mechanical design to the unmistakable sound, vibration, and character that every bike has.

Whether I'm assembling a new PC, learning about the latest technology, gaming, or admiring the engineering behind motorcycles, I'm always driven by curiosity and a desire to understand how things work.

Fitness & Discipline

Just like my passion for motorcycles and technology, fitness is a core pillar of my life. I believe that a sharp mind requires a strong, healthy body to support it.

For me, the gym isn't just about lifting weights; it's about discipline, consistency, and progressive overload. It's the physical equivalent of building a custom PC: you need the right "components" (nutrition, training, recovery), a solid plan, and the patience to see long-term results.

Balancing screen time with intense physical activity keeps me grounded, energized, and ready to tackle any challenge, whether it's debugging code or hitting a new personal record.
